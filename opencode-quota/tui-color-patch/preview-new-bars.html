New quota bar concepts — pick a direction

Thirty-two 1-row bar designs (21 cells) — every single one inside the baseline bar size, live and animated at the real speed in the real card layout. Every glyph is a full/half/eighth block or a verified font glyph — nothing melts, nothing sub-pixel. The live sidebar stays as-is until you pick one.

1 · Wave — flowing sine

The filled part is a smooth rolling wave (eighth blocks), height = energy. Empty part keeps a faint floor line.

2 · Comet — runner with trail

Dim bar; a white-hot comet head sweeps left→right leaving a fading trail behind it.

3 · Battery — chunky segments

Shaded segments (bright top / solid bottom), ghosted empty cells, and the newest segment breathing at the edge.

4 · Equalizer — multi-wave

Per-cell bars driven by layered waves (busiest option). Fill edge = the quota percent.

5 · Gradient — clean & calm

Deep-to-bright gradient with a soft traveling shimmer and a bright tip. The professional, low-key option.

6 · Radar — sweeping beam

A soft wide beam sweeps back and forth across the whole bar; the filled part is solid, the empty part lights up faintly as the beam crosses it.

7 · Loader — edge chase

Solid fill with a little 3-cell loading ladder climbing into the edge — the bar looks like it is still working on the last bit.

8 · Chevron — marching weave

Two tones woven cell by cell, the weave marching forward — the classic "in progress" pattern, done with clean half-blocks.

9 · Aurora — layered waves

Two independent waves — one on the top half of each cell, one on the bottom — drifting across the fill. Two layered surfaces in one row.

10 · Signal — scrolling sawtooth

A clean staircase ramp repeating every 8 cells, scrolling along the fill like a machine signal.

11 · Scanner — bouncing beam

A hard white block bounces left and right across the whole bar, Knight-Rider style, with a light halo. The fill sits solid underneath.

12 · Inchworm — crawling stretch

A bright worm crawls along the fill, stretching and contracting as it moves; the head leads. It exits, then re-enters from the left.

13 · String — pluck & settle

The fill is a plucked string: it swings wildly, settles, gets plucked again. A repeating, dramatic burst of motion.

14 · Ripples — drop rings

Drops land at random spots and send bright rings expanding outward through the fill — three sources, each on its own rhythm.

15 · Pulse — racing spike

A calm monitor line with a sharp spike racing through it, left to right, on repeat — with a small pre-bump and trailing dip.

16 · Duet — crossing lights

Two lights travel through each other, one from each end, and flash white in the exact moment they cross.

17 · Neon — flickering tube

The fill flickers like a tired neon tube — irregular, fast, electric. Every cell blinks on its own schedule.

18 · Hopper — bouncing light

A light hops along the bar in arcs (tall in the middle, touching down between hops), leaving a faint warm trail. The bar beneath is a slim ledge.

19 · Stream — packet flow

Evenly spaced packets of light flow rightward in a steady stream — soothing, continuous throughput.

20 · Quake — rumble bursts

Every few seconds the bar rumbles — hard brightness jitter everywhere — then settles back to a calm shimmer. Episodic chaos.

21 · PAC-MAN — baseline size

One row, like every other bar: his actual silhouette frames — ◖ (mouth open) chomping to ● (mouth closed) — running the pellet line, flashing the pellets ahead of his mouth. Both glyphs verified present in Cascadia Mono.

22 · THE MATRIX — digital rain

0s and 1s flickering in Matrix green, with a bright "drop" sweeping through. Follow the white rabbit.

23 · MINECRAFT — hearts

A row of Minecraft hearts — full, then empty. The edge heart wobbles, random hearts flicker from damage, and when you're low the whole row beats faster.

24 · POKÉMON — HP bar

The classic HP bar: top-lit sheen (green → yellow → red automatically), and every few seconds it takes damage and drains down smoothly, with a little shake.

25 · TETRIS — tetromino blocks

The fill in the seven classic Tetris block colors, the edge piece blinking like an active tetromino, and a line-clear flash sweeping through periodically.

26 · SONIC — rings & speed

A field of golden rings, all shimmering; a blue speed streak (») tears through, flashing the rings it passes. Gotta go fast.

27 · AMONG US — task bar

The segmented lime task bar: every cell is a segment with its notch, the newest lights up, and random segments pop bright like tasks being completed.

28 · OCEAN — the wave tank

A real tank, solved not faked: 12 standing modes, each an exact pair of opposite-traveling waves, obeying the deep-water dispersion law (ω ∝ √n — short waves run faster). Crests hit both walls, double up (clapotis), and re-launch; Stokes-type crest sharpening and capillary shimmer ride on top. It physically cannot desync — it's the actual solution of the wave equation.

29 · SEICHE — the tank sloshes

The bathtub phenomenon, tuned: the fundamental slosh mode dominates — the whole surface rocks end over end, one wall cresting while the other troughs, with chop riding the slide and whitecaps slamming and falling back at both ends each pass.

30 · PENDULUM WAVE — 21 bobs, 21 frequencies

Every cell is a pendulum with its own frequency, so the row shears into snaking traveling patterns and periodically snaps into perfect unison — the wave machine, in one row.

31 · DROP — the tank rings

One impulse excites all twelve modes coherently: the splash splits into two wavefronts that race out, slam both walls, refocus back at the impact point, pass through each other and re-focus again — the full transient response of the exact solution, dispersing and re-sharpening.

32 · CROSS — two seas collide

Two drop systems in flight at once, launched from different points half a cycle apart: their expanding wavefronts cross, interfere, refocus — and everything still bounces off both walls. The tank arguing with itself.
