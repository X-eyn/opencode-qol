Seiche Lab — design 29 development

A dedicated lab for the wave-tank seiche. Twenty-three variants across four axes: physics logic (rate, dispersion law, damping, mode count), water shaders (multi-stop depth gradients, glints, foam chroma), foam behaviour, and spent-space treatments (what fills the used-up part of the bar — six abstract + two open beach scenes). All cards run the real card layout and the real cell renderer. Controls: speed = phase advance per frame; frame = redraw interval — the live sidebar runs 1.00× at 120ms (defaults, as shipped).

v1 · Baseline — current 29

The exact shipped seiche: 12 modes, deep-water dispersion (ω ∝ √n), 0.5× rate, foam at 0.8 with wake memory. The reference for everything else.

v2 · Slow heave — heavier water

Fundamental slowed to 0.34× with a longer wake: the same tank but the water feels massive — long, deliberate wall-to-wall heaves.

v3 · Quick slosh — nudge a half-full tank

0.75× rate, shorter reverb, quicker foam decay. Snappier, more playful; the classic "someone bumped the table" slosh.

v4 · Shallow tank — nondispersive

Shallow-water seiche theory: ω ∝ n instead of √n, so the modes stay in tighter phase — the surface moves as one cleaner travelling mass instead of dispersing into ripples. 8 modes.

v5 · Damped + gusts — real energy budget

Friction bleeds the amplitude away, then gusts re-pump the tank. Watch the heave weaken to a simmer, then surge back to slamming whitecaps. Physically, this is what a real tank does.

v6 · 16-mode surface — fine structure

Twelve modes become sixteen: finer frequencies fill in the surface, so crests and wakes carry detail at every scale. The "microscope" variant.

v7 · Moonlight — steel + silver glints

Cold steel-blue depth gradient; coherent silver glint bands travel the surface as light catches it. Glints are phase-locked across cells — never random twinkle.

v8 · Lagoon — tropical turquoise

Bright turquoise shallows rising to mint; foam reads as white sand-froth. The warm-weather option.

v9 · Sunset — warm reflections

Indigo and violet water catching low warm light — a four-stop gradient ending in amber, with warm glints riding the swells.

v10 · Arctic — glacier water

Pale glacier blues; crests read almost frosted. Quiet, cold, premium.

v11 · Blackwater — electric edge

Near-black water where only the moving crest is lit — an electric teal edge that flickers over the top of the heave. Minimal, expensive-looking.

v12 · Bioluminescent — plankton tide

Dark water lit from within by glowing plankton: the crest glows green-cyan and the foam sparkles like disturbed bioluminescence.

v13 · Storm steel — grey weather

Desaturated slate under a grey sky: the same physics, drained of colour. The moody one.

v14 · The composite — everything on

16-mode surface + moonlight shader + glints + wake foam, all at once. If you want maximum simulation per cell, this is it.

v15 · Turquoise — baseline recolored

The exact baseline physics, recolored: same 12-mode seiche, same rate, same foam — a deep teal → turquoise → mint depth gradient instead of blue.

v16 · Spent space — slots (reference)

The current treatment for the used-up part of the bar: faint `▁` slot dashes in the bucket-tinted floor color. Compare everything below against this.

v17 · Spent space — ebb tide

The sea doesn't stop at the edge: the exact same wave field continues into the spent zone, its amplitude decaying with distance until it flattens into a low calm line. One continuous body of water across the whole bar.

v18 · Spent space — splash-over

The tank's right wall is the fill edge — so when a wave slams it, the ejecta now sprays OUTWARD into the spent zone: bright droplet specks walk past the waterline under the same ballistic deceleration, then dry up.

v19 · Spent space — ghost line

A pale eternal waterline: only the capillary ripples remain in the spent zone, travelling gently at a low height — the memory of water where the sea used to be.

v20 · Spent space — dry bed

The spent zone reads as a drying seabed: a dither band right past the water, then a fine speckled bed, with rare slow glints like damp sand catching light.

v21 · Spent space — heat haze

Keep the slot dashes, but let them shimmer: a coherent travelling brightness wave rolls across the spent zone like heat haze over dry ground.

v22 · Sandy shore — daytime beach

The spent zone becomes a beach: the shoreline gives way to a swash zone of wet sand that reaches up with every swell and slowly dries back, then a golden dune field — and a trail of footprints walking up out of the surf, drying and fading behind the walker.

v23 · Surf & wet sand — the swash zone

Same beach, more sea energy: spray flies over the shore, the wet band runs further up the sand after each boundary impact and slowly dries, and the footprints keep walking. The tide-line is real physics — it tracks the wave energy.
